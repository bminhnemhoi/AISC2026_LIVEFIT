### 2. In-Depth Analysis of Competitor Clusters

| Product / Substitute | Core Category | What It Does Exceptionally Well | Fatal Limitation for Live Commerce Operators |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok LIVE Manager** | Native Platform | Direct platform control (pinning, coupons, flash sales, native analytics) | Zero concept of an operational rundown, timing overruns, or planned vs actual script tracking. |
| **FastMoss / Kalodata** | Intelligence / Spy | Scraping competitor GMV, identifying trending products, ranking creators | Completely useless during the live broadcast; zero operational workflow assistance. |
| **TikFinity** | Interaction / TTS | Stream Deck hotkeys, sound effects, gift triggers for gaming streamers | Zero concept of product packs, commerce margins, segment pacing, or sales attribution. |
| **Shoflo / Cuez** | Broadcast Rundown | Dynamic timing calculation, live cues, collaborative director-to-crew sync | Built for TV newscasts and corporate conferences; no concept of e-commerce products, shopping bags, or GMV. |
| **Google Sheets / Excel** | Spreadsheets | Free, infinitely flexible, everyone on the team already knows how to type in it | Static document; no live clock; no dynamic overrun recalculation; doesn't log actual vs plan; cannot join to sales data. |

### 3. The Decisive Battle: LiveLift vs "TikTok LIVE Manager + Google Sheets"

To win, LiveLift does NOT need to replace TikTok LIVE Manager.

**LiveLift must prove why an operator should replace their secondary monitor's Google Sheet with LiveLift.**

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                LIVELIFT vs GOOGLE SHEETS BATTLECARD                              |
+------------------------------------+--------------------------------+----------------------------+
| OPERATIONAL CAPABILITY             | GOOGLE SHEETS / EXCEL          | LIVELIFT CONTROL DESK      |
+------------------------------------+--------------------------------+----------------------------+
| Pre-Live Rundown Formulation       | Manual cells, formulas break   | Structured ROS & Pack import|
| Real-time Presenter Cues           | Static text; hard to read fast | 5-Second NOW/NEXT Cue Band |
| Dynamic Timing Overruns            | Host must do mental math       | Automatic cascade drift    |
| Planned vs Actual Recording        | Nobody types during live       | Single-click transition log|
| Host Prompter Sync                 | Separate tab / copy-paste      | Aligned Host View projection|
| Joining Decisions to Post-Live GMV | Manual VLOOKUP (rarely done)   | Automated API reconciliation|
| Tomorrow's Plan Iteration          | Copy-paste tab; forgets fixes  | 1-Click Playbook Carry-Fwd |
+------------------------------------+--------------------------------+----------------------------+
```

#### Why Google Sheets Fails Under Broadcast Stress:
1. **The Overrun Blindspot:** If Segment 2 (Warm-up) takes 15 minutes instead of the planned 7 minutes, Google Sheets remains static. The host has no idea that the planned Flash Sale at Minute 30 is now delayed to Minute 38, or that the last three core products will be squeezed out before the broadcast ends.
2. **The "Post-Live Amnesia" Problem:** During a live stream, the assistant is frantic (managing samples, answering Zalo questions). Nobody updates the Google Sheet during the live. By the next morning, the team has no record of what was *actually* presented versus what was on the original spreadsheet.
3. **The Disconnect from Analytics:** At the end of the week, the brand owner downloads a TikTok CSV of sales and looks at the Google Sheet of planned items. Connecting the two requires painful manual reconciliation, so it is almost never done.

---

### 4. Strategic Positioning Conclusion

LiveLift's unfair advantage is becoming the **"Shoflo for TikTok Shop"**:
- **Not a TV rundown:** It understands e-commerce products, SKUs, and voucher timing.
- **Not a static spreadsheet:** It is an active operational desk that cascades timing in real time.
- **Not a platform replacement:** It sits peacefully on the second monitor alongside TikTok LIVE Manager, ingesting TikTok's post-live data to close the operational loop.
