### 3. Detailed Option Breakdown

#### Option A: Operational Evidence & Replay Layer (Strategy V2)
- *The Pitch:* "The unforgeable operational ledger for livestream commerce."
- *Strengths:* Intellectually rigorous; solves epistemic integrity; protects against false claims.
- *Fatal Flaw:* It is an **internal architecture disguised as a customer product**. Merchants do not pay for epistemic purity; they pay to run a better, more profitable live broadcast.

#### Option B: Commerce Run-of-Show & Live Operations Desk (Recommended Pivot)
- *The Pitch:* **"The Smart Rundown for Live Commerce: Plan the show, keep the host on cue, manage timing overruns, and improve tomorrow's live in 3 minutes."**
- *Strengths:*
  1. Attacks an immediate, universal, daily pain point (replacing messy Google Sheets + Zalo).
  2. Delivers instant visual value during the live broadcast via the 5-second NOW/NEXT command band and dynamic cascade drift calculation.
  3. Provides an effortless 3-minute post-live review that automatically suggests concrete adjustments for tomorrow's plan.
  4. 100% functional on Day 1 without requiring ByteDance API keys.
  5. Retains all the architectural robustness of Option A (append-only ledger, provenance, bitemporal safety) as quiet internal infrastructure.

#### Option C: Enterprise Causal Optimization Suite (Legacy Evolution)
- *The Pitch:* "A/B testing and algorithmic revenue optimization for high-volume live rooms."
- *Strengths:* Caters to large brands spending millions on live advertising; high potential contract value.
- *Fatal Flaw:* The market for live rooms with $>500$ concurrent viewers that can achieve statistical significance is tiny ($<1\%$ of shops). It completely abandons the mainstream SME live commerce market.
