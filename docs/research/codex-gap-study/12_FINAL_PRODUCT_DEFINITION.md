# Final recommended product definition

Research date: **2026-10-05**. Repository: `rebuild/livelift-next`. This is research and a proposed product strategy, not implementation or proof of market demand. The [V3 master roadmap](../../roadmap/LIVELIFT_V3_MASTER_ROADMAP.md) governs future work; these reports supply evidence.

| Required field | Decision |
|---|---|
| PRODUCT NAME | **LiveLift Commerce Operations Desk** |
| ONE-LINE POSITIONING | Execute a timed commerce show, recover when it drifts, and turn actual execution into a better next rundown. |
| PRIMARY ICP | Recurring Vietnamese merchant teams and small livestream agencies with a dedicated operator and host, initially one active room. |
| SECONDARY ICP | Creator + assistant with repeated, promotion-heavy shows; only if logging burden is acceptable. |
| JTBD | When a commerce show departs from its plan, help the team protect important cues and coverage, record what actually happened, and prepare the next show without reconstructing everything. |
| CORE PROBLEM | Future timing commitments, human coordination and actual operational changes are scattered across native controls, spreadsheets and messages. |
| CORE WORKFLOW | Prepare timed plan → operate/recover → record actual transitions/reports → review variance → approve coherent next-plan changes. |
| CORE DIFFERENTIATOR | Constraint-aware recovery connected to traceable actual execution and selected adaptation, with no required TikTok API. Conditional workflow contribution, not first-in-market invention. |
| P0 | Manual products, timed segments/anchors/minima, transparent projections, operator commands, actual event history, Plan vs Actual, selected-change clone, deterministic offline rehearsal. |
| P1 | Validated Host View (P0.5), simple handoff and repeated operational patterns; optional entitled catalog/session/post-data enrichment. |
| LATER | Single-room agency standards/training; demand-led multi-room or one additional platform; separately preregistered experiments. |
| NOT BUILDING | Broadcast/video/chat clone, GMV/traffic BI, AI scripts/diagnosis clone, host AI practice clone, unsupported auto pin/promotion control, general OMS/PIM, broad cross-platform control. |

## Primary ICP comparison

| Segment | Recurring timing pain hypothesis | Dedicated operator / data capture | Purchase / scope fit | Choice |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Solo creator | Low–medium, varies by show | Often none; another screen costly | Low initial fit | Not primary. |
| Creator + assistant | Medium where promotions and product rotation matter | Possible | Good secondary test | Secondary. |
| Merchant team | Medium–high with recurring planned campaigns | Usually identifiable lead/operator | Best initial one-room fit | Primary recruitment. |
| Small agency | High if repeating client/host playbooks | Clear role split and review need | Strong candidate, avoid multi-room scope | Primary recruitment. |
| Large agency | Potentially high | Rich staffing/data | Procurement/permissions/SLAs broaden scope | Defer. |

These are hypotheses, not an interview census. Recruitment should prioritize demonstrated repeat shows, multiple time commitments and actual recovery incidents; reject participants who only want GMV analytics.

## Why not TikTok?

Use TikTok for broadcast, platform execution, scripts, analytics and replay. Add LiveLift only if the team cannot efficiently manage its planned timing commitments and next-show operational changes with its actual native tools. This bounded residual gap still needs an account walkthrough. Native AI sequence and script guidance are not reasons to use LiveLift. [T3: LIVE Guide / shopping bag preparation](https://seller-vn.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=8405487868626705&lang=en); [T4: AI-generated insights during LIVE](https://seller-vn.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=4898053111744273).

## Why not Sheets?

Sheets is a legitimate competitor. LiveLift must reduce formula maintenance, actual-history reconstruction and recovery time while preserving cheap/fast setup. If a good sheet performs equally well, the broad desk is not justified.

## Why not Shoflo?

Shoflo already solves mature rundown timing and presenter coordination. Commerce-specific constraints and approved operational changes might save additional work; compare a commerce template first. If no added advantage appears, use a template/integration instead of a new timing product. [C3: Shoflo timing basics](https://learn.shoflo.tv/en/articles/1261400-timing-basics); [C5: Shoflo changelog](https://feedback.shoflo.tv/changelog).

## Why not FastMoss / Kalodata?

They address commerce intelligence and live/post monitoring. LiveLift would address a team's agreed plan, recovery and operational memory. If the actual job is product discovery, creator research or sales intelligence, those tools and TikTok are the appropriate choice. Do not claim they lack real-time capabilities. [C12: Kalodata knowledge base](https://www.kalodata.com/knowledge.html); [C13: FastMoss LIVE detail example](https://www.fastmoss.com/en/live/detail/7443208632554031903).

## Why not Zalo + spreadsheet?

Only replace repeated timing/next-cue messages when a shared projection improves host comprehension and reduces operator work. Keep ordinary chat. Familiarity and no extra setup may make Zalo + sheet the better answer for simple shows.

## Honest commercial promise

Promise help with executing and improving the team's show process, not higher GMV. Evidence of operational time savings or cue reliability can support later qualified claims. No price, willingness-to-pay or sales superiority was established in this task.

## Multi-platform classification

| Classification | Scope | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| CORE NOW | Platform-independent manual show method: products, timing, human transitions, review and next plan | No connector is needed to operate the team's plan. |
| ARCHITECTURAL PREPARATION | Separate session/room/provider identity, observation source/time and metric units | Avoid binding operational truth to one platform; no speculative plugin factory. |
| FUTURE BET | One additional officially supportable connector or agency workflow after repeated demand | Each platform's data/control and identity must be verified independently. |
| DISTRACTION | Immediate TikTok/Shopee/YouTube/Facebook commerce OS with unified automatic control | Different APIs/permissions, unproven demand and lowest-common-denominator controls overwhelm the wedge. |

Multi-platform portability strengthens the product only when retained users actually need it. An offline manual plan alone is not evidence of working live integrations across platforms.
