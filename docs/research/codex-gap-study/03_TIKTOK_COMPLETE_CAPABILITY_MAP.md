# TikTok complete LIVE capability map

Research date: **2026-10-05**. Repository: `rebuild/livelift-next`. This is research and a proposed product strategy, not implementation or proof of market demand. The [V3 master roadmap](../../roadmap/LIVELIFT_V3_MASTER_ROADMAP.md) governs future work; these reports supply evidence.

## Classification contract

All cited pages were accessed **2026-10-05**. **CONFIRMED — VIETNAM** refers to published official VN capability; account eligibility and actual usability were not tested. **CONFIRMED — OTHER MARKET** remains market-specific. **OFFICIAL BUT RESTRICTED** denotes documented partner features needing permissions. **UNVERIFIED** is an unsupported marketing or historical assertion; **NOT FOUND** is a bounded search absence; **INFERENCE** is our product interpretation. A selected-account rollout is not general availability.

## Native workflow reconstructed

**Before LIVE:** choose products and reuse a set; order the shopping bag; prepare native scripts and eligible promotions; schedule an event; check equipment or use practice features. Selected accounts additionally receive AI product/sequence advice.

**During LIVE:** host broadcasts through native desktop/mobile tools; assistant/moderator uses Manager/Console; native product, coupon, giveaway and billboard controls operate in that environment; live activities and Suggestions support performance decisions. Host script prompts and commerce metrics already exist.

**After LIVE:** session analytics, replay/comments, pin/promotion markers, diagnosis, product and traffic analysis and native exports support review. This is more than a metrics-only product.

The hypothesized remaining work is an explicit team-owned planned-vs-actual show, hard deadlines, recovery tradeoffs and approved next-show adaptation. We did **not** find a formal future-segment hard-anchor cascade or selected-change rundown clone in these reviewed sources. This is **NOT FOUND**, not a verified assertion about every TikTok UI or upcoming feature.

## Market and rollout traps

- The VN LIVE Guide and AI insights sources describe account-selective experiences; do not put their presence into a blanket “all accounts” promise.
- The AI Practice Room guide is recent (15 September 2026) and includes VN among early markets. Historical “TikTok only rehearses AV” conclusions are obsolete.
- LIVE Manager and Scripts documentation versions describe editing/settings behavior differently. An account walkthrough should settle script refresh/overwrite and toggle behavior before any integration design depends on it.
- The US Data Compass source establishes partner analytics in that market. It does not establish Vietnamese studio permissions, APIs or multi-room control.
- Screenshot currencies/date formats are examples. They do not establish local payout, offer eligibility or date interpretation.

## Claims explicitly withdrawn

Withdraw “TikTok lacks scripts,” “TikTok lacks product ordering,” “TikTok has no actionable live advice,” “TikTok has no replay context,” “TikTok has no simulator,” and “TikTok analytics are only after LIVE.” Each is contradicted or materially qualified by the cited official material.
