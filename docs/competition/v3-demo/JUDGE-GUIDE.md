# LiveLift in one minute (for judges)

> Written for the V3 freeze. TikTok Login Kit identity (V4) and LIVE Intelligence (V7) came later; current status is in
> the [root README](../../../README.md).

## What it is

The operating desk around a LIVE. Not a TikTok replacement, and not connected to TikTok.

**Create → Prepare → Operate → Review → Next LIVE**

## Who it is for

The operator or live assistant who works beside a host on a TikTok Shop LIVE. Small teams
that today coordinate through spreadsheets, notes and chat.

## The problem

During a LIVE the plan slips: a segment overruns, a promised moment gets close. The team
needs to see what is on now, what is next, why it matters, and what to do about it, without
reading a spreadsheet. Afterwards, what really happened is usually remembered, not recorded.

## How to start

1. Open Home and read the five-step strip.
2. **Simulator → Fall collection rehearsal**. No account needed.
3. Start it and press **Apply step** twice. The desk shows a 20:12 commitment at risk and the
   recovery that protects it.
4. Finish the rehearsal and open **Review**.

Short on time? Home → **Finish the review** opens a completed rehearsal.

## What is real, and what is simulated

| | |
|---|---|
| **SIMULATED** | A rehearsal on a virtual clock, kept in the browser. Badged everywhere. Never counts as real history. Everything in the walkthrough is this. |
| **REAL** | A show recorded in the shared room under a signed-in operator. Needs a deployed server. |
| **Not connected** | TikTok analytics, realtime engagement, pin or promotion control, platform verification. See **Integrations**. |

## Why Review matters

Review puts the plan beside what was recorded, and keeps three things apart:

- **reported**: the operator's word, not platform confirmation;
- **unknown**: the outcome lacks confirmation; a report or attempt may still exist, and this is not a failure;
- **observed in this show**: from recorded commands only.

It then lets the operator choose which changes carry into the next plan. It makes no claim
about sales: one show is "not a recurring pattern".

## What to check for honesty

- Every rehearsal is labelled SIMULATED, including on the Review page.
- A missing price shows "Not entered", never 0.
- The Integrations page says what is not supported.
