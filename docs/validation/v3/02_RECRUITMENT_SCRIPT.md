## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

This document provides the standardized operational recruitment kit, bilingual communication scripts (Vietnamese and English), screening questionnaires, briefing packets, legal agreements (NDA and Informed Consent), and onboarding checklists for the LiveLift V3 Product Validation Study.

### 1.1 Core Principles of Study Positioning
1. **Neutral Operational Framing:** Outreach messaging strictly avoids e-commerce marketing buzzwords, "AI magic," or speculative claims of sales/GMV lift. The research is positioned clinically as an:
   > *"Operational research study on live broadcast schedule management, real-time pacing drift recovery, and post-session rundown auditing."*
2. **Respect for Studio Workflow:** Recruitment targets operational pain points directly experienced by studio staff: frantic Zalo messaging during overruns ("cháy giáo án"), missed flash deal windows, formula corruption in spreadsheets, and painful post-show time reconciliation.
3. **Ironclad Commercial Privacy:** Prospective merchant teams are guaranteed total data isolation. The study never inspects sales figures, profit margins, supplier agreements, customer PII, or internal TikTok Shop analytics.

## 4. Participant Briefing Packet (What to Expect)

### 4.1 Study Overview & Time Commitment
* **Total Session Duration:** Exactly **75 to 90 minutes**.
* **Environment:** Conducted either in-person at your studio facility or remotely via synchronized dual-feed Google Meet.
* **Format:** Hands-on operational simulation comparing two rundown management workflows across two 15-minute mock broadcast trials.

### 4.2 Hardware & Technical Setup Checklist
Prior to session start, the participant studio must prepare:
1. **Operator Station:**
   - 1 Desktop PC or Laptop (Windows or macOS) with Google Chrome (Version 120+).
   - High-speed Internet ($\ge 30\text{ Mbps}$ download/upload).
   - Mouse and full keyboard.
2. **Host Station:**
   - 1 On-camera workspace with ring light or studio illumination.
   - 1 Auxiliary Tablet (iPad or Android tablet) or smartphone mounted in the host's direct line of sight (beside the camera lens).
   - Working audio headset or external microphone for clear voice capture.
3. **Recording Authorization:**
   - Facilitator will record operator screen and host camera feed via OBS Studio for timestamp verification. No private personal data will be captured.

### 4.3 Simulation & Wizard-of-Oz Disclosure
In accordance with academic research standards, participants are informed:
* The LiveLift software tested in this study is a **Phase 0 Research Prototype**.
* Certain automated calculation engines (downstream deficit forecasting, recovery suggestions) and tablet synchronization are actively assisted by a background simulation controller (Wizard-of-Oz protocol).
* The purpose of the trial is to evaluate whether the **visual workflow and operational decision structure** reduce operational mistakes and stress compared to spreadsheets.

### 4.4 Honorarium & Payment Terms
* **Compensation:** **1,500,000 VND** per Operator-Host pair (or $60 USD equivalent).
* **Disbursement:** Transferred via instant bank transfer (VietQR) or cash immediately upon completion of the post-test interview.

## 6. Logistics, Scheduling & Onboarding Checklist

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                  VALIDATION TRIAL ONBOARDING TIMELINE                             |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  [ T - 48 Hours: Technical Verification & Calendar Lock ]                                         |
|  [ T - 24 Hours: Baseline Template Dispatch & Catalog Review ]                                    |
|  [ T - 15 Minutes: Pre-Flight Hardware, OBS & Screen Calibration ]                                |
|  [ T + 00 to T + 90 Min: Trial Execution & Debrief Interview ]                                    |
|  [ T + 95 Min: Honorarium Disbursement & Data Archive ]                                           |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 6.1 Phase 1: 48 Hours Prior to Trial (T - 48h)
- [ ] Confirm participant attendance via phone / Zalo with both Operator and Host.
- [ ] Verify test venue (In-person studio address or Google Meet link dispatched).
- [ ] Assign unique anonymized subject keys (e.g., `P03-OP`, `P03-HOST`, `TEAM-03`).
- [ ] Execute digital NDA via DocuSign or pre-signed PDF agreement.
- [ ] Determine counterbalanced condition order from master matrix:
  - `[ ] A -> B` (LiveLift first, then Baseline).
